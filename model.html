The Four-Step Blueprint

01

The Free Build

After your application is accepted, our team gets to work. We design, code, and optimize a custom website for your brand. We do not use slow templates or clunky builders. Every site is hard-coded for maximum speed and performance.

Your Cost: $0.00

02

The Review & Approval

Within a few weeks, we send you a live staging link to view your new site. You can click around, test the speed, and see it on mobile. If it doesn't align with your vision, you walk away. No hard feelings, no invoices.

Your Risk: Zero.

03

Domain & Activation

When you give the green light, we push the site live. You retain full ownership of your brand. You simply purchase your own domain name (e.g., yourbusiness.com) and any necessary backend services (like email hosting). We handle the technical connection.

Your Cost: Standard domain registrar fees (usually $10-$20/year).

04

The Ad-Supported Partnership

Instead of charging you $3,000+ for the website and $100/mo for maintenance, we host and maintain the site for free forever. In exchange, CODOT integrates highly curated, non-intrusive ad spaces into the layout. We collect the ad revenue to cover our development and hosting costs. When your business grows and gets traffic, we both win.
